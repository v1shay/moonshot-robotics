Damaged Field Repair Station - Individual STL Components
========================================================

These files split the damaged field repair station into separate reusable STL assets.
STL stores mesh geometry only; rain/fog/light/spark effects are represented as physical meshes.
Most assets are composed of multiple closed shells; intersections are intentional for easy printing/import.
